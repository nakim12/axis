# Axis evaluation results

Generated by `scripts/run_evaluation.R` from the frozen dataset in `data/eval/` (see its MANIFEST). Do not edit by hand.

Settings, fixed before any results were computed: 252-day lookback, monthly rebalance on the last trading day, 10 bps per unit of turnover, 35% weight cap (raised to 1/n when fewer names are eligible). Sharpe and Sortino use the 13-week T-bill rate. Confidence intervals come from a stationary block bootstrap (mean block 21 days, 2,000 resamples) that resamples the same days for every strategy.

Sharpe ratios are after costs unless labeled. A difference from equal weight whose interval includes zero is not distinguishable from noise.

## Forecasts: ARIMA vs a random walk

Generated by `scripts/run_forecast_eval.R`. Rolling origins every 5 trading days from 2015-01-01 (or two years after listing), a 504-day training window of log prices, horizons of 1, 5, 21 trading days. `auto.arima` re-selects the order every 13 origins; coefficients are re-estimated at every origin. The baseline is a random walk (tomorrow's price equals today's). Errors are in log-price space.

RelMAE is ARIMA's mean absolute error divided by the random walk's on the same points; below 1 means ARIMA was more accurate. Significance uses a two-sided Diebold-Mariano test on absolute errors with the Harvey-Leybourne-Newbold correction, then a Benjamini-Hochberg correction across all tickers and horizons.
